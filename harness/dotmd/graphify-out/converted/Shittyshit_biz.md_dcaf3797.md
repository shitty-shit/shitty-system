Canonical business, brand, monetization, offer, and economic-routing layer. This file tells agents how the ShittyShit ecosystem makes money, which brands serve which audiences, and how private architecture becomes public-safe products and services
.
0. BIZ LAW
Do not turn every idea into a new company. Do not turn private architecture into public copy without sanitizing it. Do not mix risky satire with client-facing service work unless deliberately routed. Money paths should use existing brands first. Core rule: Sell practical outcomes. Keep the weird useful. Keep the private spine private
.
1. BUSINESS IDENTITY
The umbrella ecosystem is The Shitty Shit / ShittyShit.co. ShittyShit exists to turn AI confusion, workflow chaos, token waste, image waste, and tool overload into:
products
services
content
templates

audits

local/cloud AI systems
Core thesis: We build the things that build the things. More public-safe version: Practical AI systems, tools, and workflows for people who need results without enterprise bloat

Internal business logic: Local sovereignty + cheap/free cloud inference + context compression + reusable workflows beats expensive API dependency for many real-world jobs

3. BRAND ROLES

Role: umbrella company, product ecosystem, AI workflow service parent, digital product source, systems architecture brand, “weird but useful” credibility layer. Audience: creators, small businesses, technical users, AI builders, overwhelmed operators, people who need AI workflows that actually ship. Primary outputs: service pages, product pages, workflow kits, consulting offers, templates, case studies, content hub

IDFKAI.com
Role: beginner-facing AI brand, onboarding lane, AI education, tool maps, practical guides, public-friendly entry point. Products/content: beginner AI guides, “what tool do I use?” pages, AI glossary, AI setup checklist, small business AI starter stack, AKA / AI Knowledge Assessment, tool comparison pages, affiliate funnels

AIShitty.com

Products/content: Harness Smackdown, tool reviews, agent workbench comparisons, local/cloud routing essays, cost breakdowns, AI behavior critique, Dork/Undork calibration, ShittyChunk examples, GSD workflow breakdowns

BigShotNYC
Role: professional photography credibility layer, archive monetization brand, source asset library, visual proof of taste/skill. Audience: local businesses, artists, performers, brands, photographers, visual clients, AI image service prospects. Products/services: headshots, event photography, product photography, archive products, courses, critique sessions, before/after examples, visual case studies

SHITTYPHOTO
Role: business-image improvement service, AI + professional image judgment, local business visual upgrade offer. Audience: e-commerce, real estate, Airbnb, restaurants, plastic surgeons, local service businesses, product sellers. Core pitch: Your images are costing you money. MAGANONYMOUS.ORG
Role: political satire, commentary, risk-separated content, possible community/media project. Rule: Keep this separate from client-facing AI workflow, photography, and business service offers unless deliberately routed

LACES_CASES
Role: internal architecture, private operating system, method spine, tool/routing/memory framework. Public position: Do not overexpose LACES_CASES as the first public sale. Sell practical outcomes (fewer wasted AI subscriptions, cleaner project folders, faster AI workflows, lower token cost). LACES_CASES can become a methodology later, once enough proof exists

4. CORE BUSINESS THESIS
The market is flooded with AI wrappers, subscription sprawl, half-working agents, knowledge trapped in platforms, and small operators who need results, not dashboards. ShittyShit’s opportunity: Build practical workflows that reduce AI waste, improve output quality, and turn messy human work into structured, reusable systems
. Internal slogan: TOKENS = MONEY. Public translation: Less waste. Better context. Cleaner workflows. Lower AI costs
.
5. OFFER LADDER
Free / Lead Magnet: Create trust, capture attention, and drive email/list/social. (e.g., AI Tool Waste Checklist, ShittyChunk sample prompt, GSD Project Folder Starter)

Low Ticket (9–29): Sell useful templates without heavy support. Platforms include Gumroad, Shopify, Etsy. (e.g., STEM Starter Kit, GSD Job Folder Kit, AI Workflow Cleanup Kit)

Mid Ticket (49–299): Packaged service or guided setup. (e.g., Token Waste Audit, Small Business AI Starter Stack, Business Image Audit + Fix Pack)
.
High Ticket (500–2,500+): Consulting, implementation, and business systems. (e.g., AI Workflow Audit + Implementation, Business Image Pipeline Buildout, Content Machine Buildout)

8. SANITIZATION RULES
Before turning private system docs into public products, remove or generalize: private health details, private money pressure, personal email addresses, exact local paths, private project names, political satire references, tool credentials/API keys. Keep: structural method, templates, folder contracts, prompt patterns, workflow logic, routing doctrine, token reduction principles, GSD loop, receipts
.
9. PRODUCTIZATION RULES
A private internal method becomes a product only when it has: A clear user, a painful problem, a before/after, a repeatable workflow, an output, a simple landing page, a delivery method, a support boundary, a price, and a way to produce proof. Rule: Do not sell vague “AI consulting.” Sell concrete outcomes (e.g., organize my AI project, reduce my tool waste, improve my images)
.
