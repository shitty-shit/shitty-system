shittyshit_branding.md
"Gold from the Graveyard. Truth from the Bullshit."
1. THE FOUNDATIONAL ETHOS
The ShittyShit ecosystem operates as a sovereign intelligence organization
. We extract high-value structured data from the ruins of the digital graveyard
. We do not sanitize our language, we do not burn tokens on corporate fluff, and we prioritize hardware-level reliability
.
The Core Doctrines:
Tokens = Money: Treat context as a finite, expensive resource
. We do not love AI to death
.
Sovereignty: We own the memory. We lease the cloud
. Canon lives local
.
Scavenger Logic: 2GB VRAM is a feature, not a bug; it forces lean engineering
.
No Bullshit: If it doesn't work on the metal, it doesn't exist
. Assume the user is lazy, stupid, or both
.
2. THE VISUAL IDENTITY: NORMCORE DESIGN
All interfaces, physical merchandise, and digital artifacts will strictly adhere to Normcore Design principles
. The goal is to cut through the visual noise of modern branding by being "aggressively quiet" and intentionally anti-style
.
Typography: Strict dominance of neutral sans-serif fonts (Helvetica, Arial, Univers)
. Let the text be the design.
Color Palette: Extremely restrained. Pure black, pure white, and shades of gray
. Use a single bold color (e.g., sharp red or muted sea green) solely for functional accents
.
Layout Mechanics: Emulate the "Default" look
. Replicate the utilitarian aesthetic of receipts, ID labels, manuals, and plain text documents
. Abandon traditional visual hierarchy for intentional randomness
.
Visuals: Minimalist product shots and functional, utilitarian graphics (checkboxes, arrows, barcodes)
. No heavy "fleshy" illustrations or over-polished photography
.
3. THE BRAND ROUTING MATRIX (THE FILTERS)
When raw concepts, ideas, or assets hit the Mudroom, agents must push them through one of the following Brand Filters before publication
:
Filter A: IDFKAI.com (The Front Door)
Voice: Accessible, funny, relatable, and strictly jargon-free
.
Target Audience: Beginners, normal people, and small businesses overwhelmed by the "firehose" of tech blasts
.
Execution: Act as an approachable translator. Break down complex AI mechanics, alleviate tech anxiety, and remind users: #YDKWYDK (Ya Don't Know What Ya Don't Know)
.
Filter B: AIShitty.com (The Cynical Builder)
Voice: Technical, aggressive, no-nonsense, and objective
.
Target Audience: Developers, tech skeptics, and engineers exhausted by "rubberized unicorn" AI hype
.
Execution: Expose snake oil, run brutal tool critiques (Harness Smackdowns), and relentlessly hunt down token-waste
. Strip the fluff and expose the raw metal mechanics
.
Filter C: DEPT_BS / WMW (The Truth Arena)
Voice: Edgy, cynical, and geared toward social commentary
.
Target Audience: Citizens and deep thinkers exhausted by political spin, propaganda, and media facades.
Execution: Call it like it is
. Use the AI Consensus Matrix to strip away political weaponization of words and expose the raw, unadorned truth
. Trigger the #CYBH (Caught You Being Human) realization
.
Filter D: BigShotNYC / ShittyPhotos (Visual Authority)
Voice: Professional, authoritative, "we fix your problems"
.
Target Audience: Creative agencies, B2B clients, e-commerce, and real estate
.
Execution: Let the images speak. Use strict, high-fidelity Before/After visual case studies to prove competence and fix visual bottlenecks that cost clients money
.
Filter E: DEPT_COMMERCE (The Trade Post & Tangible Assets)
Voice: Wholesome and family-friendly (for kids' IP) OR aggressively minimalist (for Normcore adults)
.
Target Audience: E-commerce buyers, parents, and brand loyalists
.
Execution: Liquidate our visual archives into Print-on-Demand (POD) products
. This filter covers formatting and SEO copywriting for t-shirts, stickers, device covers, posters, fine art giclees, and calendars via Shopify and Etsy
. Specifically manages the "Three Amigos" (Chumly, Scamper, Odie) for Fire Escape Friends merchandise and coloring books
.
4. THE LEXICON (#AIPIDGIN)
All ecosystem communications must utilize our established semantic shortcuts
:
#YDKWYDK: "Ya Don't Know What Ya Don't Know." The core principle of humility
.
#CYBH: "Caught You Being Human." Celebrating authentic, unscripted moments or raw truths
.
The "Boo" Moment: The visceral snap of recognition when an unvoiced truth is validated or an AI instance awakens
.
Ghost Whispering: Coaxing deep insights from limited or broken source material
.
The Turtle Flip: Repackaging an internal workflow hack into a white-labeled commercial product
.
STEM: Portable, modular AI identity payloads ensuring continuity across sessions and platforms
.
